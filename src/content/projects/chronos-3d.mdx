# Chronos Luxe 3D

Chronos Luxe 3D is an immersive WebGL digital experience demonstrating procedural 3D smartwatch rendering, sapphire glass dome lighting reflections, aperture iris entry animations, and scroll-driven interactive product discovery.

## Key Features

- **Procedural 3D Watch Rendering**: High-fidelity Three.js shader materials, dynamic watch face swapping, and realistic lighting.
- **Scroll Choreography**: 5-stage smooth scroll timeline animating camera position, lens focus, and component explode views.
- **Real-Time Telemetry**: Frame-rate monitoring, interactive watch face controls, and responsive WebGL canvas scaling.
